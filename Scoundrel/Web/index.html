Room 1

Scoundrel

Original game by Zach Gage and Kurt Bieg, © 2011

How to play

You are a scoundrel delving into a dungeon built from a deck of 44 cards. Clear every card while staying alive.

The cards

Monsters are clubs and spades, 2 to Ace. Their number is the damage they deal. J = 11, Q = 12, K = 13, A = 14.

Weapons are diamonds, 2 to 10. Picking one up equips it and throws away your old weapon along with its kills.

Potions are hearts, 2 to 10. They heal that much, up to your maximum HP (20 unless changed in Settings).

Rooms

Each room shows 4 cards. Tap cards one at a time to deal with them. Once you've handled 3, the room refills to 4 from the deck. The card you left behind stays.

Fighting

Under each monster you see two options:

  • Fist: fight bare-handed and take the monster's full value as damage.
  • Sword: fight with your weapon and take only the monster's value minus the weapon's value. The monster ends up on your weapon's stack.

Weapons dull: once a weapon has a kill, it can only hit monsters equal to or weaker than its last kill. The badge on your weapon shows that limit. Tapping the card itself picks the sword when it can hit, and fists otherwise.

Potions

You can drink only one potion per room. A second potion in the same room is wasted.

Fleeing

Before touching a full room, you may flee. All 4 cards go to the bottom of the deck and you get a fresh room. You can't flee two rooms in a row.

Scoring

Survive by clearing the whole deck. Your score is your remaining HP. If you're at full health and your last move was drinking a potion, its value is added on top.

Die when HP hits 0. Your score is your HP minus the value of every monster still in the dungeon.

Tips

  • Kill big monsters with your weapon first. Small kills dull it fast.
  • Sometimes fists are smarter, to save your weapon for a bigger threat.
  • Count what's left: the top bar shows cards and monsters remaining.

Settings

Changing maximum health starts a new dungeon.

Abandon this dungeon?

Your current run will be lost.
